## 2. Have the program Terms and Conditions been published? What do they say?

### Takeaway
No. As of 26 Nov 2026 — eleven days after the "on or before November 15, 2026" target — no Terms and Conditions page exists on canton.foundation or www.canton.network, and the announcement page is unchanged. Every detailed eligibility question (countries, age, teams, AI content, wallet, lockups, tax) is therefore unanswered by any official source; only the general commitments in the announcement apply.

### Cited Findings
- [PRIMARY] The live announcement page fetched on 26 Nov 2026 is word-for-word the same as the saved copy; it still says the Terms "are expected to be published on or before November 15, 2026" and contains no link to a Terms document. — [Canton Foundation announcement](https://canton.foundation/introducing-the-canton-community-research-grants/)
- [PRIMARY] canton.foundation's Yoast post sitemap (35 posts, last modified 2026-11-25) lists no Terms/grant-terms post. The only posts dated after 4 Nov are: six new members (6 Nov), Bit Dynamics dev-fund project (12 Nov), open standards for equity ownership (12 Nov), Nomura/Boostry core nodes (20 Nov), and the security auditor call (lastmod 25 Nov). The announcement post's own last-modified stamp is 2026-11-04T16:40:15Z, i.e. never edited. — [canton.foundation post sitemap](https://canton.foundation/post-sitemap.xml)
- [PRIMARY] canton.foundation's page sitemap (21 pages) has no Terms page; the only legal-type pages are `/privacy-policy/` and `/responsible-disclosure/`. `/grants-program/` exists but is the Protocol Development Fund page (last modified 2026-08-07), unrelated to research grants. — [canton.foundation page sitemap](https://canton.foundation/page-sitemap.xml)
- [PRIMARY] www.canton.network's sitemap contains only one matching URL, the blog excerpt of the announcement; no Terms URL. — [canton.network sitemap](https://www.canton.network/sitemap.xml)
- [SEARCH] Web searches for "Canton Community Research Grants Terms and Conditions" returned only the announcement and unrelated university/municipal grant terms. — [canton.network blog result](https://www.canton.network/blog/introducing-the-canton-community-research-grants)
- [PRIMARY] What the announcement commits the Terms to cover: "eligibility, identity verification (KYC), sanctions screening, disclosure requirements, and how grants are made and paid." "No grant will be made before the Terms and Conditions are published, and every grant will be subject to them." — [Canton Foundation announcement](https://canton.foundation/introducing-the-canton-community-research-grants/)
- [PRIMARY] Rules that already bind participants via the announcement, independent of the Terms:
  - KYC and sanctions screening before any grant; a signed grant agreement with the Foundation; "The Foundation may decline to make a grant to anyone, for any reason, at any stage."
  - Grant-receipt disclosure: "Recipients will be required to disclose that they received a grant, as described in the Terms and Conditions."
  - Sponsorship disclosure: "Please also disclose any relevant sponsorships, financial interests, or commercial relationships associated with your work."
  - Originality/authorisation: "Please share only original content that you created or are authorized to represent."
  - Engagement integrity: "Artificial or purchased engagement will be disregarded."
  - Payment: "Grants are denominated in Canton Coin, and their USD value may fluctuate"; grants are subject to "applicable legal, tax, and payment requirements."
  - Membership: "You don’t need to be a Canton Foundation member to be considered."
  - Contact: "If your work is under consideration, the Foundation will contact you privately about the Terms and Conditions, verification, and grant documentation."
  - Legal character: "It is not an offer, a contest, or a promise to make any grant"; the Foundation "may modify, suspend, or end the program at any time."
  — [Canton Foundation announcement](https://canton.foundation/introducing-the-canton-community-research-grants/)

### Inferences
- Because "no grant will be made before the Terms and Conditions are published," no November grant can have been paid yet, and the Terms must appear before any payout. Participants should re-check canton.foundation/news and @CantonFdn before and after 30 Nov.
- The Terms may be delivered privately ("the Foundation will contact you privately about the Terms and Conditions") rather than, or before, being posted publicly. The announcement's wording allows both readings; it says they will be "published."
- The announcement does not state a time zone for "November 1 and November 30, 2026" or for "December 4, 2026." Publishing and sharing comfortably before the end of those days in UTC and in US time zones avoids the ambiguity.
- A recipient needs to be able to pass identity verification under their real name and to receive Canton Coin (which implies a Canton-compatible wallet or custody arrangement), but no official source yet says which wallet, or whether there is a lockup.

### Gaps
- Not stated anywhere official: eligible/excluded countries, minimum age, individuals vs teams/companies, treatment of AI-generated or AI-assisted content, language requirements, whether one author may submit several works, payment wallet type, vesting/lockups, tax withholding or reporting, the exact wording required for grant-receipt disclosure.
- Whether the Terms were distributed through a channel not searchable from here (Discord `discord.gg/canton`, Telegram `t.me/CantonNetwork1`, Foundation Slack, email) is unknown; those hosts were not reachable.
- No explanation from the Foundation for the missed 15 Nov target was found.

## 7. Other Canton programs a newcomer could confuse this with

### Takeaway
Three other Foundation mechanisms are aimed at entirely different people: the Protocol Development Fund (milestone-funded engineering via GitHub pull requests), Featured App status (production applications earning network rewards), and the 24 Nov security-auditor request for quotes (professional audit firms). None accepts articles or videos, and none uses the X hashtag route.

### Cited Findings

**Protocol Development Fund (the site's "Grants Program" menu item)**
- [PRIMARY] The canton.foundation navigation item "Grants Program" (`/grants-program/`) is the Protocol Development Fund, not the research grants. It funds "core R&D, spanning development and performance work, security enhancements, audits, reference implementations, and tooling and infrastructure contributions", plus DeFi app liquidity seeding and critical infrastructure. — [Grants Program page](https://canton.foundation/grants-program/)
- [PRIMARY] How it works: proposals are submitted by opening a pull request in `github.com/canton-foundation/canton-dev-fund` using the PR template; the Tech & Ops Committee evaluates and approves; "Funding is allocated quarterly in Canton Coin and is tied to clear milestones"; non-members need a champion on the Tech & Ops Committee; "There is no fixed cap"; funding is not retroactive in the sense that it "is released when corresponding milestones… have been reached"; "It is not a token incentive program." — [Grants Program page](https://canton.foundation/grants-program/)
- [PRIMARY] Funding source: "Super Validators voted and approved to programmatically reallocate 5% of future Canton Coin supply" (CIP-82 and CIP-100); Canton Coin "was fair-launched, and so no centralized pool or treasury was set aside for grants." The launch post is dated February 19, 2026 and quotes Executive Director Melvis Langyintuo. — [PDF launch post](https://canton.foundation/canton-foundation-launches-protocol-development-fund/)
- [PRIMARY] A first report on the fund and a completed-project post (Bit Dynamics, Canton DevKit) exist on the Foundation site. — [post sitemap](https://canton.foundation/post-sitemap.xml)

**Featured App Request**
- [PRIMARY] "Anyone can write an App for the Canton Network. If you are within 2 weeks of production, you can start the process of becoming a Featured Application. This will be sent to the Tokenomics Committee for review." The form asks for the applying institution, application name, Party ID, featured app type (Basic Featured App / Featured Asset-Moving App / Featured Asset), and a Standalone Super Validator sponsor. "Deadline: 14 calendar days from this announcement." Selection by the Technology & Operations Security Subcommittee with core contributors including Digital Asset. ## 8. Consolidated participation checklist for the November 2026 round (derived only from the findings above)

### Takeaway
Two hard steps and several conduct rules are all that is officially required; everything else is discretionary and the Terms are still missing.

### Cited Findings
- [PRIMARY] Step 1: "Publish your work. Your article, analysis, or video must be publicly published between November 1 and November 30, 2026." — [announcement](https://canton.foundation/introducing-the-canton-community-research-grants/)
- [PRIMARY] Step 2: "Share it. Post a link on X, tag @CantonFdn, and include #CantonResearchNov26." "Work shared by December 4, 2026 will be reviewed in connection with November." — [announcement](https://canton.foundation/introducing-the-canton-community-research-grants/)
- [PRIMARY] Sharing is optional in principle ("the Foundation may also consider work it finds on its own") and "does not create any right to a grant." — [announcement](https://canton.foundation/introducing-the-canton-community-research-grants/)
- [PRIMARY] Conduct rules: original work you created or are authorised to represent; disclose sponsorships, financial interests and commercial relationships; no artificial or purchased engagement. — [announcement](https://canton.foundation/introducing-the-canton-community-research-grants/)
- [PRIMARY] If shortlisted: private contact from the Foundation; KYC; sanctions screening; signed grant agreement; later, public disclosure that a grant was received; possible public highlighting by the Foundation. — [announcement](https://canton.foundation/introducing-the-canton-community-research-grants/)
- [PRIMARY] Payment in Canton Coin, up to 50,000 CC per month across all recipients, possibly less or nothing. — [announcement](https://canton.foundation/introducing-the-canton-community-research-grants/)
- [PRIMARY] As of 26 Nov 2026 the Terms and Conditions are not published on either official site. — [post sitemap](https://canton.foundation/post-sitemap.xml); [page sitemap](https://canton.foundation/page-sitemap.xml)

### Inferences
- With four days left in the publication window on 26 Nov, the binding constraint is the 30 Nov publication date; the X share can follow until 4 Dec, but doing both by 30 Nov removes time-zone risk.
- Good practice visible in existing entries and consistent with the rules: put a disclosure line in the piece itself (holdings, employer, sponsorship, AI assistance if any, and "submitted to the Canton Community Research Grants"), cite sources, and separate own calculations from third-party figures.
- Keep the X account reachable for private contact (the Foundation says it will reach out privately) and be prepared for real-name identity verification.

### Gaps
- Whether direct messages must be open, or how the Foundation contacts people, is not stated.
- All Terms-dependent items listed in section 2's gaps.

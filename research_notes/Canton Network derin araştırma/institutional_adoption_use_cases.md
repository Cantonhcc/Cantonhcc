# Institutional Adoption and Real-World Use Cases on the Canton Network (as of 26 November 2026)

Method note for the report writer: canton.network and canton.foundation pages were read directly (primary). Every other host was unreachable from this machine, so press/third-party items rest on web-search result summaries of the linked URL, not on a full read of the article; these are marked "(via search)". Status labels used: ANNOUNCED / PoC or PILOT (completed or running) / LIVE TRANSACTION(S) (real assets, small choreographed sets) / PRODUCTION (generally available service).

## 1. Tokenized collateral and repo: Broadridge DLR, the Tradeweb/working-group Treasury financing trades, Euroclear / World Gold Council, margin

### Takeaway
Broadridge DLR is the only Canton-linked workflow with sustained, published, very large volumes (USD 7.5-7.7tn a month in 2026), and it is counted by trackers as "represented" assets on Broadridge's own platform. The Tradeweb-executed working-group trades (July 2025 to January 2026, plus July 2026) are real but small, choreographed sets of transactions that proved weekend atomic settlement, collateral reuse and cross-border multi-currency repo; none has published notional values.

### Inferences
- The working-group series is best described as a staged set of live demonstrations: counts of trades are tiny (1, then 5, then unspecified "sets"), no notionals are published, and participants themselves use "PoC" language. It proves feasibility (weekend atomic DvP, reuse, cross-currency), not adoption at scale.
- Almost all of Canton's headline dollar volume is one application (Broadridge DLR) in one market (US Treasury repo, largely intra-group/bilateral dealer repo). DLR daily volume has been flat to slightly down since October 2025 (385bn to 345bn), so 2026 "growth" in headline value is not coming from DLR.

### Gaps
- No published notional size for any working-group transaction. Not found.
- Whether DLR transactions are sequenced through the public Global Synchronizer or run on Broadridge's own private Canton synchronizer was not established from sources I could read; trackers classify DLR as "represented" (not transferable outside the platform), which suggests the latter, but treat as unverified.
- Clearstream: no Canton-specific collateral pilot found in 2025-26 (only the historical Deutsche Börse D7/Daml link). World Gold Council: nothing after the 2024 pilot found.

## 2. DTCC: tokenization of DTC-custodied securities on Canton

### Takeaway
This is Canton's most important 2026 milestone: announced 17 Dec 2025 after an SEC no-action letter, first limited production trades on 15 Jul 2026, and the DTCC Tokenization Service (DTS) launched on 28 Oct 2026 on Canton and on DTCC's own private Besu network. It is live but young: about 20 signed DTC participants and a few dozen eligible securities at launch, operating under a time-limited no-action framework, and DTCC is explicitly multi-chain.

## 3. Bank and FMI platforms built on Canton or Daml: public network versus private ledgers

### Takeaway
Most of the "bank platform" names (GS DAP, HSBC Orion, BNP Paribas Neobonds, Deutsche Börse D7, Broadridge DLR, Versana) are private, permissioned ledgers built on Digital Asset's Daml/Canton technology; Canton marketing counts them as "Canton-connected". Evidence of activity on the public network (Global Synchronizer) exists for HSBC (pilots), Lloyds, LSEG DiSH, BNP Paribas (own node in DTCC trades), Société Générale, Goldman Sachs (DTCC list) and Tradeweb, but mostly at pilot or first-transaction stage.

### Inferences
- "Assets on Canton" figures of USD 3.6tn to 6tn-plus cannot be stocks of tokenized assets (rwa.xyz shows about USD 0.32-0.35tn represented); they are most plausibly cumulative or monthly flow figures, mostly DLR repo turnover. A report writer should never present them as assets outstanding.
- Transaction counts (1.7m a day) and dollar volumes measure different things: a large share of transactions is likely Canton Coin transfers, reward minting and app activity, whereas dollar volume is DLR. Neither demonstrates broad institutional usage on its own.
- Firm counts (650, 830) mean "connected" entities, which includes validators, wallets and small crypto firms; no breakdown by type or activity is published.

### Gaps

- No independent audit of "$9T monthly" or of the 830-firm count.

## 9. Why institutions pick Canton, why some choose alternatives, and named sceptics

### Takeaway
Institutions cite sub-transaction privacy, atomic cross-application settlement, governance by regulated peers and predictable costs. Sceptics argue that Canton is closer to a consortium system than a blockchain, that its headline numbers are one repo platform's record-keeping, and that bank-run chains have a poor history; several large users (DTCC, JPMorgan, Visa) are in fact multi-chain.

### Cited Findings
**Stated reasons for choosing Canton**
- Privacy versus transparent chains: Visa said the transparency of blockchains "can clash with privacy expectations financial institutions operate under" — [canton.network](https://www.canton.network/news/visa-teams-with-canton-network-to-preserve-blockchain-privacy)
- Franklin Templeton (Roger Bayston): Canton lets it "deliver a private blockchain option alongside the interoperability clients expect" — [canton.network](https://www.canton.network/canton-network-press-releases/franklin-templetons-benji-technology-platform-expands-to-canton-network)
- Summit takeaway: institutions need "privacy, governance, and predictable economics"; participants control who sees transactions, no single entity controls critical infrastructure — [canton.network](https://www.canton.network/blog/canton-summit-2026-takeaways)
- 24/7 intraday financing: Marex's Steven Hood: "you can do a repo for four hours. You can do a repo over the weekend" — [canton.network](https://www.canton.network/blog/onchain-collateral-repo-tokenization)
- Regulatory fit: SEC no-action letters for DTC's tokenization service (Dec 2025) and for Canton Coin (Nov 2026) — [canton.network](https://www.canton.network/canton-network-press-releases/sec-recognizes-canton-coin-as-a-digital-commodity-providing-additional-regulatory-clarity-for-institutional-adoption)
- Design notes cited by critics and supporters alike: Canton does not publish all transactions for all validators to verify against a shared global state, and allows removal of historical data (pruning), which helps with EU privacy rules (via search) — [DL News](https://dlnews.com/articles/defi/canton-network-ceo-addresses-criticism-from-crypto-ideologues/)

**Why some choose alternatives / multi-chain behaviour**
- DTCC's service runs on its own private Besu network as well as Canton, with more networks (Solana reported) planned for 2027 (via search) — [Altcoin Buzz](https://www.altcoinbuzz.io/dtcc-tokenized-securities-service-canton-network); [Blockchain Reporter](https://blockchainreporter.net/what-is-dtcc-tokenization-service/)

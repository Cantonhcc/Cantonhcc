## 1. Digital Asset: founding, leadership, ASX CHESS, pivot to Daml/Canton, funding, headcount

### Takeaway
Digital Asset (Digital Asset Holdings, LLC, Delaware; HQ New York) was founded in 2014, was led by Blythe Masters from March 2015 to December 2018 and has been led by co-founder Yuval Rooz since 19 March 2019. After the ASX cancelled its Daml-based CHESS replacement on 17 November 2022 (A$245–255m write-off), the company re-centred on the Canton Network (announced 9 May 2023); its latest round is US$355m led by a16z crypto on 11 June 2026 at a reported US$2bn valuation, extended to US$365m on 21 July 2026.

## 2. Canton Network timeline (2023 – November 2026)

### Takeaway
Canton was announced on 9 May 2023 with 30 named participants, piloted on TestNet (results published 12 March 2024), and went live as a public network when the Global Synchronizer and Canton Coin launched on 1 July 2024; CC began exchange trading on 10 November 2025 and received an SEC staff no-action letter on 3 November 2026. Super Validator governance by CIP pre-dates MainNet (first CIPs approved 26 January 2024).

## 3. Organisations: Digital Asset vs GSF vs Canton Foundation vs Linux Foundation vs Hyperledger/LFDT

### Takeaway
The Canton Foundation is the same legal entity as the Global Synchronizer Foundation (GSF) — a Delaware non-stock, non-profit membership corporation registered 24 June 2024, claiming 501(c)(6) status, set up with Linux Foundation support — and the September 2025 change was "a change in name only". Digital Asset is one Premier member/one board seat and the main code contributor; the Linux Foundation is the host/partner; Splice lives in Hyperledger Labs, with a ratified but apparently incomplete plan to move Splice, Daml and Canton repositories under the Foundation.

## 5. Super Validators: who, how admitted, weights, on-chain voting (DSO)

### Takeaway
"Super Validator" covers two different things: about 56–60 organisations hold SV reward rights, but only 13 SV nodes run by 11 organisations hold the on-chain votes, and votes are one-per-node, not weight-based. With 13 nodes the threshold is 9. The Foundation's node hosts 46% of all SV reward weight on behalf of 40-plus beneficiaries.

**How one becomes an SV**
- By a Governance CIP that "defines Super Validator rights owners and their weights", sponsored by one SV and endorsed by a second (one endorser suffices if the author is an SV), then approved by a 2/3 vote and applied on-chain via the `SRARC_AddSv` / "Update SV Reward Weight" actions — [CIP-0000](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0000/cip-0000.md); [Docs-SVgov](https://docs.canton.network/overview/reference/sv-governance-reference.md)
- Early tiers: T1 = weight 10, T2 = 3, T3 = 1, T4 = 0.5; later CIPs use bespoke weights (e.g. Zero Hash 7.5, Kaiko 6.5, Republic 6, Paxos 8) — [CIP-README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/README.md)
- Milestone-based ("ghost"/escrow) admission: an `extraBeneficiary` party is set up by the Foundation or another approved SV node operator at the maximum earnable weight; the representative SV does not mint; rewards go to the Unclaimed Rewards pool until milestones are confirmed. Milestones for DTCC, Visa, a16z crypto and Nomura are confidential and reviewed by the Tokenomics / Accountability committees or "SVAC" — [CIP-0083](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0083/cip-0083.md); [CIP-0109](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0109/cip-0109.md); [CIP-0123](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0123/cip-0123.md); [CIP-0130](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0130/cip-0130.md)
- CIP-0045 (14 Feb 2025): any SV with reward weight above 2.5 must operate its own SV node within 6 months or have its weight cut to 1; motivation: a "free rider problem" on the "GSF multitenant" node — [CIP-0045](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0045/cip-0045.md)
- CIP-0105 (2 Mar 2026): voluntary locking of lifetime SV rewards determines forward weight — at start, lock 70% → earn 100% of weight (Tier 1), 45% → 60% (Tier 2), 35% → 40% (Tier 3); Tier-1 requirement falls to 65%/60%/55% in years +1/+2/+3; unlocks vest at 1/365.25 per day; Digital Asset-1/-2 and Cumberland-1/-2 are aggregated per organisation. CIP-0127 (23 Oct 2026) adds on-chain enforcement — [CIP-0105](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0105/cip-0105.md)
- CIP-0111 (23 Apr 2026): weight reductions are evaluated by the Tokenomics Committee (may delegate to the Accountability Committee), ratified by SV off-chain vote, then an on-chain vote within three days; unminted coupons of failed "Ghost SVs" are burned — [CIP-0111](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0111/cip-0111.md)
- CIP-0114 (16 Apr 2026): time-bound SV programme for Digital Asset Treasury companies, with a US$100m AUM threshold, quarterly evaluation and escrowed release — [CIP-0114](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0114/cip-0114.md)
- Sanctions rule: SV members must not propose or approve any SV that fails an OFAC or sanctions check and must remove such an SV from their configuration immediately — [Bylaws](https://canton.foundation/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Bylaws-Approved-20260526.docx.pdf)

**On-chain voting (DSO = "Decentralized Synchronizer Operations"/"Operator")**
- The DSO is a decentralized Daml party hosted across all SV nodes; tolerated faults f = floor((n − 1)/3); required votes = ceiling((n + f + 1)/2); the DSO party's protocol-level confirmation threshold is ceiling(n × 2/3) — [Docs-SVgov](https://docs.canton.network/overview/reference/sv-governance-reference.md)
- Any SV can create a `VoteRequest` with a `voteBefore` deadline and optional `targetEffectiveAt`; outcomes are Accepted, Rejected or Expired; any SV can execute a passed action (CIP-0064 delegateless automation) — [Docs-SVgov](https://docs.canton.network/overview/reference/sv-governance-reference.md)
- Action types: Add SV, Offboard SV, Grant/Revoke featured app right, Update SV reward weight, Set DSO config, Set Amulet (Canton Coin) config; routine actions (mining rounds, SV onboarding confirmation) are confirmed automatically — [Docs-SVgov](https://docs.canton.network/overview/reference/sv-governance-reference.md)
- CC–USD rate and fee parameters use the **median** of values published by each SV — [Docs-SVgov](https://docs.canton.network/overview/reference/sv-governance-reference.md)
- "Its SV node carries the same voting weight as any other SV" (of the Foundation) — [Docs-SVgov](https://docs.canton.network/overview/reference/sv-governance-reference.md)
- CIP-0051 (26 Mar 2025): votes change individual config fields; proposals expire if fewer than 2/3 have voted; votes can be changed until effectivity; early rejection at 2/3 reject; optional "immediate effectivity" at 2/3 accept — [CIP-0051](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0051/cip-0051.md)
- Ordering layer: "requires more than 2/3 of Super Validators to be operational for the network to advance" — [Docs-policies](https://docs.canton.network/overview/reference/gsf-policies.md)

### Inferences
- With n = 13: f = 4, required votes = ceiling(18/2) = 9, and DSO confirmation threshold = ceiling(8.67) = 9. Five nodes withholding can block any action.
- Digital Asset (2 nodes) plus Cumberland/DRW (2 nodes) hold 4 of 13 votes — below both the passing threshold and the blocking threshold on their own, but one additional node short of a blocking minority. By reward weight they hold 8.3% and 7.1% respectively.
- 155.375 of the 381.605 weight (40.7%) sits in entries labelled ghost/escrow (79.25 on the Foundation node, 49.125 on MPCH, 27 on Five North) — i.e. not yet earned and conditional on milestones. Computed from the config file.
- Because voting is per node, the ~45 organisations whose SV rights are hosted on the Foundation, MPCH or Five North nodes have reward rights but no direct on-chain vote; their influence runs through the off-chain CIP vote and through the host (the Foundation "votes according to the direction of its Members").
- The homepage figure of "64 Super Validator Nodes" cannot be node operators (there are 13); it most likely counts SV reward identities/beneficiaries.

### Gaps
- Whether the off-chain CIP vote (GitHub Team Voting by "designated representatives of the Super Validator rights owners") is one-vote-per-rights-owner or weight-based is not stated in CIP-0000; individual vote tallies are in PDFs/mailing lists not read.
- The config file is the reference used at onboarding and may lag live on-chain weights; the live SV Network Status page loads data dynamically and could not be read.
- "SVAC" is not expanded in the CIP texts read.
- Woodside AI appears on the SV list although CIP-0059 (weight 10) is marked Withdrawn; unexplained.

## 6. Validators: number, eligibility, onboarding, SV sponsorship

### Takeaway
Validators number roughly 700–750 (Foundation counter: 742; SEC letter: 700+), but joining MainNet/TestNet is permissioned in practice: a corporate applicant needs Tokenomics Committee approval, a sponsoring Super Validator, an IP allowlist entry adopted by the SVs and a one-time onboarding secret. Individuals are steered to 52 approved Node-as-a-Service providers.

### Cited Findings
- Counts: 742 total validators (Foundation homepage, data by The Tie); "over 700 Validators" as of 30 Sep 2026 (SEC letter, via search); "over 200 validators" in June 2025; "more than 1000" per Kaiko — [CF home](https://canton.foundation/); [GSF post](https://canton.foundation/how-a-blockchain-is-governed-is-critical-heres-why/); [Kaiko](https://www.kaiko.com/resources/index-in-focus-inside-out-canton-ecosystem-coin) [search snippet]

DevNet needs no committee approval but still needs the allowlist — [Docs-policies](https://docs.canton.network/overview/reference/gsf-policies.md); [Validators page](https://canton.foundation/validators/)

## 7. The CIP (Canton Improvement Proposal) process

### Takeaway
CIPs move Draft → Proposed → Approved → Active/Final, are discussed on the public cip-discuss mailing list and as GitHub pull requests, need a sponsoring and an endorsing Super Validator, and pass on a 2/3 vote of SV representatives within 10 days; on-chain adoption then needs a 2/3 SV on-chain vote. The index lists 121 CIPs up to CIP-0132 (56 Final, 44 Approved, 8 Active, 3 Proposed, 3 Replaced, 1 Obsolete, 5 Withdrawn, 1 Rejected).

### Cited Findings
- CIP-0000 authors: Wayne Collier, Stanislav German-Evtushenko, Amanda Martin, Taras Katrichenko, Itai Segall; created 2024-01-03; approved 2025-03-03; derived from Bitcoin's BIP-0002 — [CIP-0000](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0000/cip-0000.md)
- Stages: Draft (anyone may join cip-discuss and author) → editor assigns number and type → Proposed (needs "support from at least two Super Validators") → vote on cip-vote list (private membership, public archive), 10-day deadline → Approved by "a 2/3 majority vote in favor", cast by "designated representatives of the Super Validator rights owners… using GitHub Team Voting" → Active (no on-chain step) or Final (once 2/3 of SV node operators implement it on-chain by vote or software adoption). Other statuses: Deferred, Rejected, Withdrawn, Replaced, Obsolete — [CIP-0000](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0000/cip-0000.md)
- Types: Standards Track, Governance, Tokenomics, Process, Informational. Deadlines to reach a vote after introduction: Standards Track 3 months; Governance, Tokenomics, Process 1 month; Informational 2 weeks — [CIP-0000](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0000/cip-0000.md)
- Venues: lists.sync.global groups cip-discuss, cip-vote, cip-announce, supervalidator-ops, tokenomics-announce; repository github.com/canton-foundation/cips; results announced by Foundation staff — [CIP-0000](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0000/cip-0000.md); [Docs-policies](https://docs.canton.network/overview/reference/gsf-policies.md)
- CIP editors: Stanislav German-Evtushenko (SBI), Taras Katrichenko, Wayne Collier (Digital Asset), Itai Segall (Digital Asset), Amanda Martin (Foundation COO) — [CIP-0000](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0000/cip-0000.md)
- A hard-fork CIP requires de facto adoption by the whole community, SVs and Validators — [CIP-0000](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0000/cip-0000.md)
- **Rejected:** CIP-0033 Add Strange Pixels as Tier-4 SV, weight 0.5 (14 Dec 2024) — [CIP-0033](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canton-foundation/cips/main/cip-0033/cip-0033.md)

- KYC on Canton is layered rather than chain-wide: sanctions screening at the membership/SV layer, corporate vetting at the validator layer, and KYC/permissioning left to each application and asset issuer; CC itself is freely tradable on exchanges that apply their own KYC.
- The SEC position depends on the continued truth of "no central party". Facts in these notes that bear on it: Digital Asset has 2 of 13 node votes and one board seat, but authors most CIPs and receives ~92% of Development Fund payments; a change in those facts (or in SV concentration) could weaken the basis for the relief.
- Canton's press release overstates the letter relative to how the legal/trade press describes it ("confirms… not a security" vs "no legal conclusion").
- The CFTC's role is indirect: the March 2026 joint interpretive release supplies the "digital commodity" category; no CFTC-specific action on CC was found.

### Gaps
- The SEC letter and Digital Asset's incoming request were not read directly (sec.gov not reachable); all details are from press summaries.
- No source found for any specific FCA, Bank of England, Japan FSA, MAS, Korean FSC or Hong Kong SFC determination on CC's classification.
- Which EU national competent authority received the MiCA notification is not stated in the parts read.
- No legal opinions (other than the SEC submission) or litigation were found; absence of evidence, not proof of absence.

## 11. Governance criticisms and responses

### Takeaway
Critics argue that Canton is permissioned in everything but name, that control and early CC issuance are concentrated in Digital Asset, DRW and a small, mostly US set of institutions, and that the Foundation's neutrality is unproven; Canton's answers are structural (2/3 voting, one board seat for Digital Asset, board reform, SV locking, the Development Fund, licence-free synchronizers) and rhetorical (Rooz's dismissal of "crypto ideologues"). The primary data collected here supports parts of both sides.

### Inferences
- Data supporting the critics: on-chain votes sit with 13 nodes run by 11 organisations; the Foundation's node carries 46% of SV reward weight; Digital Asset authors most CIPs and received ~92% of Development Fund payouts to August 2026; validator entry is allowlisted; large-institution SV terms are confidential; DRW-linked parties appear as Digital Asset founder-backer, round lead, two SV nodes (Cumberland), a board seat (Zuehlke) and the CEO/board seat of Canton Strategic Holdings (Wendland), in whose financing the Foundation itself invested.
- Data supporting Canton: no single organisation can pass or block a vote alone (9 of 13 needed; DA and DRW hold 2 each); CC had no pre-mine or ICO and the Foundation funds itself from dues and one SV weight; the board has 19 directors from 19 organisations with a DTCC chair; governance artefacts (CIPs, configs, by-laws, committee lists) are public; several 2026 measures (board cap/terms, locking, weight-reduction process, licence removal) move in the decentralising direction.
- The Foundation's neutrality claim is strongest on formal voting rights and weakest on agenda-setting and execution capacity, where Digital Asset remains the dominant author, maintainer and paid contractor.
- The Foundation both administers SV escrow for dozens of rights holders and holds a large CC treasury plus an equity stake in a listed CC treasury company — a combination of referee, custodian and market participant that critics could reasonably question; no source was found addressing this directly.

### Gaps
- No independent, quantified study of CC holdings by entity was found; "early SVs accumulated a large amount" is asserted, not measured, in the sources reached.
- The Big Whale, DL News, Unchained and Cointelegraph pieces were not read in full; quotations are from search summaries.
- No published conflict-of-interest policy for the Foundation was read (CIP-0082 says a later CIP "must detail… conflicts policy"; CIP-0100 covers appeals but has no dedicated conflicts section beyond escalation).
- No response from the Foundation was found to criticism of confidential SV milestones or of the Tharimmune investment.
